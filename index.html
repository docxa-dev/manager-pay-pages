ManagerPay - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ManagerPay is operated by Docxa. Contact: docxadev@gmail.com, WhatsApp +91 94278 10142.

1. Scope

ManagerPay is an offline-first business operations app. In this version all data you enter is stored locally on your device. The app does not send your business data to a server and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Data we handle

  • Account details: username, display name and a salted password hash (never the plaintext password).
  • Business records: locations, staff names, phone numbers, employee codes, optional identity-document labels and masked numbers with expiry dates.
  • Financial records: collections, cash handovers, settlements, salaries, advances, expenses and an audit log.
  • Attachments: optional photos or PDFs you choose to attach, stored only as local paths.
  • Device identifier: a random per-install ID used only to label local sync operations.

3. How data is used

Only to provide app features: recording collections, tracking cash custody, managing staff and tasks, and producing reports. We do not sell or share your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local SQLite database in the app's private storage. Passwords are hashed with a per-account salt. Backups you create are written to the app's private files and can optionally be AES-encrypted with a passphrase you supply; we cannot recover it.

5. Permissions

The release build requests no dangerous Android permissions. Photo and PDF selection uses the system picker. Internet access is used only in debug builds for Flutter tooling.

6. Data deletion

Delete your account any time under More - Delete business & account (owner) or More - Delete my account (staff), or email/WhatsApp us with the subject ManagerPay account deletion. We confirm within 7 days.

7. Children

ManagerPay is a business tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We will update this page when data practices change, for example when backend sync or cloud backup is introduced, and update the Google Play Data safety declaration accordingly.
