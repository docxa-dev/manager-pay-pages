ManagerPay - Account Deletion

You can delete your account instantly inside the app:

  1. Open ManagerPay and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete business & account (owner) or Delete my account (staff).
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting an owner account permanently removes all collections, transfers, staff records, documents and reports for that business. Deleting a staff sub-account removes that login and its sessions.
